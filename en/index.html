OgTech is a distributor of oxygen generators based on PSA, VSA and VPSA technology. We assess capacity, oxygen purity, delivery pressure, operating profile and overall energy use to find the right solution. VSA and VPSA enable highly energy-efficient on-site production. The plant can be combined with liquid oxygen (LOX) for backup supply and peak demand.

OgTech Core. Gas expertise, built in.

OgTech Core is being developed to bring control, communication and operating data together in a single, clear overview. The goal is better coordination across the oxygen system, easier work for operators and a stronger basis for detecting anomalies and optimising operations.
